    Verification and limitations
    • Test direct-origin isolation, forged headers, revoked/expired certificates and copied cookies.
    • Test Safari passkey enrollment and fresh authentication, session expiry, CSRF, logout and repeat login. Provider account recovery must not bypass admission or certificate binding.
    • Invites are expiring and single use. Registration creates a new account only; no account recovery or existing-user passkey replacement route is provided.
    • APIs, terminals, logs, previews and sandbox management remain disabled. Their ownership checks and WebSocket revocation are separate integration work.
    • Provider role removal alone is not immediate local-session revocation. Remove the provider role and restart irv to revoke current sessions; every restart/deploy signs everyone out.
    • Operator accounts, deployment permissions and device security are separate controls. This page does not establish AAL3 compliance.
